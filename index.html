IMPERIAL ARCHIVES // FIELD MANUAL

1. SYNDICATE STRUCTURE

You command an underworld syndicate. Your goal is to gather credits, amass Infamy, and conquer the bounty ladder by assembling an army of up to 2,000 unique galactic combatants.

2. COMBAT ARCHETYPES & PASSIVES

  • Force Adept (Sith/Jedi/Inquisitor): 15% chance to leech 30% damage dealt as HP + Force Deflection shield.
  • Mandalorian: Inherent Beskar plating blocks 25% incoming damage and reflects flat damage back.
  • Hunter / Assassin: 25% Critical strike rate (dealing 2.2x damage) and occasional Thermal Detonators.
  • Trooper / Commando: 20% chance to unleash a double-tap blaster volley.
  • Smuggler / Slicer: Siphons credits on hit and commands higher sale prices in Shadow Market auctions.

3. RECRUITMENT PROBABILITIES

Each Cantina bracket rolls across 4 tiers with fixed chances: 50% (Lowest), 30% (Mid), 15% (High), and 5% (Apex tier).

4. APEX TARGET CAPTURE & ASCENSION

Every 5th Bounty Board encounter is an Apex Target. Slaying an Apex target immediately subdues and adds them to your Barracks with cybernetic enhancements pre-installed. Ascending resets the ladder but bestows permanent +5% stat boosts to all future recruits.

5. COMMODITY ARBITRAGE & EXPEDITIONS

Commodity prices shift every 30s by up to 300%. Buy low, sell high. Expeditions grant large stockpiles of resources alongside elite rescued operatives.
